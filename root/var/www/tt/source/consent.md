Tandem Tales is a website that lets you play an interactive story with another
person or an AI agent. It is free and anonymous, but we record the choices you
make and questions you answer for research.

#### Who can play?

Only people who are 18 years old or older and can read English should play. 
These stories do not contain adult content, but this platform is not approved to
collect data from people under 18 years old.

#### Is there any cost to me?

No, playing on this website is free.

#### How do I play?

The story is shown in text on the website. You and your partner will take turns
making choices for what your characters do next. When it is your turn, you will
see a list of options, and you should click the one you want to choose.

If you are the *player* you will control one character. If you are the
*game master* you will control all the other characters. The game master can
stop the player from doing actions but cannot force the player to do anything.

There are no right or wrong choices. You should make whatever choices you want.
We do want to remind you that your partner might be another person, so please be
considerate when making your choices.

#### How long does it take to play?

Most people finish a story in under 15 minutes. How long it takes depends on
which story you play, how long you and your partner take to make choices, and
which choices you make. There is a 3 minutes limit for each choice, but a game
could last many turns or only a few turns depending on your choices.

#### Do I have to finish the story?

You can stop playing at any time by leaving this website or closing your web
browser, but if you leave before the story ends, you may not get credit for
playing. Sometimes we ask questions at the end of the story. If you leave before
answering the questions, you may not get credit for playing.

If you take more than 3 minutes to make a choice, or if you leave or close this
website accidentally before the end, you may not get credit for playing.

If your partner leaves before the story ends, you *can* still get credit for
playing.

#### Can I talk to my partner?

No, you cannot send messages to your partner, and they cannot send messages to
you. The only way you can interact with your partner is by choosing what to do
next from the list of choices.

Unless you were told in advance, there is no way to know if your partner is a
person or an AI agent.

#### What data do you collect?

We collect the name of the story you play, the choices you and your partner
make, and the ending of the story. If questions pop up during or after the
story, we record your answers.

Play is *anonymous*. We will never ask for your name, address, or other
information that could identify you. You do not need to make an account on this
website. We might ask for general information like your age range and level of
experience with video games.

If you are playing as part of a task, we may record your ID number so we can
give you credit for playing. For example, if you came here from
[Prolific](http://prolific.com), or if you are playing as part of a classroom
assignment, we might record your ID number so you can get credit for playing.

Our website records the IP address of everyone who visits, but we will not
release your IP address or associate it with your stories.

#### What kind of data do you release to the public?

We release [data sets](/data), which are groups of stories that show what choices
the players made. The data may include the answers that people gave to questions
they were asked during or after the story. The data *does not* include the names,
ID numbers, or IP addresses of the people who played those stories.

#### Are there any content or trigger warnings?

Some stories have fantasy violence, like one character attacking another with
a sword. Stories never contain sexual or explicit content.

#### Are there any other risks?

You or your partner might make choices that cause the story to take a long time.
For example, if you walk around in circles many times, the story could take a
long time to finish.

Stories are designed to always have an ending, but in rare cases it may be
possible for you to find a mistake in the story that prevents it from ending.

#### Terms and Conditions

By playing on this website, you agree that you are at least 18 years old, that
you are playing of your own free will, and that you are aware of the minimal
risks. You agree that anonymous data about your stories may be released to the
public.
