---
title: Tandem Tales
---

Tandem Tales is a research platform that lets you play a short interactive story
with another person or an AI agent. One of you is the *player*, who controls one
character in the story. The other is the *game master*, who controls the other
characters and the environment.

It's free and anonymous to play, but we record the stories people make for
research. You don't need to make an account, and we'll never ask you for your
name or other identifying information. The stories you make and questions you
answer might be included in one of our data sets. Check out our
[consent page](/consent.html) for answers to most questions.
